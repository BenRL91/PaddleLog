# UPA amateur competition checks — reviewed October 2, 2026

The old amateur entry checked model approval only and always raised a blanket partial-coverage warning. It now checks the recorded configuration and exposes individual evidence.

## Official evidence

- UPA-A Rulebook v0.9, August 2026: §§1.2.1–1.2.2 distinguish general amateur/professional rules from professional Appendix B; §3.2.2 permits USAP-listed paddles, encourages UPA-A approval, requires continued compliance after alteration, prohibits specified damaged surface conditions, and permits inspection. https://upaa.unitedpickleball.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/V0.9-8.24.26-UPA-A-Rulebook-.pdf
- Specifications v26.6, July 28, 2026: §§3.1–3.4 describe dimensions, configured weight, thickness, weighting, surfaces, and accessories. The preface expressly describes professional-event scope. https://upaa.unitedpickleball.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/UPA-A-Paddle-Specifications-and-Testing-Requirements-V26.6-7.28.26.pdf
- PPA public handbook §2.A.ii adopts USAP rules except amendments. https://www.ppatour.com/about/player-handbook/

## Behavior

Current USAP or UPA-A model approval is accepted. Shared 17-inch length and 24-inch combined dimension limits are evaluated. Accessory types and physical inspection conditions have evidence. Both accepted approval lists can be explicitly matched in the UI.

The newer general rulebook cross-references specifications whose preface states professional scope. Until the governing body clarifies amateur applicability, professional-only weight, thickness, surface, and weighting-method failures become RULES_NEED_VERIFICATION, retaining candidateCode and the measured evidence. They do not become amateur violations. Cases within these candidate limits do not trigger the former blanket coverage warning. This does not establish absence of an amateur maximum or automatically permit skins/sliders.

No complete legal guarantee is made. Physical inspection, installation quality, performance testing, exact variant matching, and event-specific requirements remain outside a remotely recorded check. Rule freshness still expires after 30 days.
