This is a private preview. Subscriptions cannot currently be purchased. Displayed target prices are planning information, not an offer to charge you.

PaddleLog Pro is an app-specific subscription. It does not grant Pro access to other LeewayPB apps. Each app may offer a separately priced subscription. A future LeewayPB Pro ecosystem plan will be a separate, higher-priced option covering participating apps; its price and availability have not been announced. Sharing a LeewayPB account does not combine subscriptions.

Free accounts can maintain three active paddles and export their personal backups. Existing paddles and history remain visible and editable if Pro expires. A fourth active paddle or reactivation above the limit requires Pro; inactive and retired records remain available.

When enabled, subscriptions will be purchased through Apple, with the localized price and billing period shown before confirmation. They renew automatically unless cancelled in Apple Account settings. Cancellation stops future renewal; paid access lasts through its expiration. Removing the app or deleting your PaddleLog account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Final legal operator/contact information, support and refund information, and the applicable EULA must be completed before public distribution or real purchases. These preview terms do not claim to be a finalized commercial agreement.
