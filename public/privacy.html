PaddleLog privacy

Private preview · by LeewayPB · Updated October 2, 2026

PaddleLog stores your paddles, specifications, configuration, sessions, maintenance, optional player profile, saved location names, imported reference entries, and uploaded photos using its existing ChatGPT Sites database and object storage. Records are scoped to an internal LeewayPB account ID. Shared LeewayPB sign-in uses Supabase Auth; this private preview also has an owner-only hosting access gate.

Form drafts, unfinished saves, and pending photo uploads stay in this browser’s storage. They are not encrypted against someone with access to your unlocked device. They remain after signing out so you can resume on this device, under the same account. Clearing browser data removes these local recovery copies.

To recover from accidental changes, PaddleLog keeps previous confirmed library snapshots. The configured policy retains the 20 most recent copies, weekly copies for eight weeks, monthly copies for six months, and the oldest copy. Cleanup runs after successful saves. Copies with uncertain dates are preserved. The pre-migration library also remains a recovery copy. Deleted or edited library records may remain in these copies until retention cleanup or account deletion. Snapshots reference photos rather than duplicating the image files.

PaddleLog records daily operational counts of successful save attempts, failed saves, sync conflicts, backup failures, cleanup failures, load failures, and reported device draft-storage failures. These counters contain event codes, dates, and an internal account ID; they do not contain notes, photos, ratings, payment details, or authentication tokens. Old counters are pruned to a 90-day window when new activity is recorded. Hosting and authentication providers may retain additional operational logs under their own policies.

PaddleLog adds no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Location names and profile details are optional user entries; the app does not request GPS location or contacts. Third-party rating and reviewer integrations require their own authorization and licensing before being enabled.

All plans can export records or a photo-inclusive backup within the displayed file limits. You can download photos, restore available recovery copies, and delete your PaddleLog account without a subscription. Account deletion removes active app records, app recovery copies, app operational counters, and account photos. It does not automatically delete the shared LeewayPB sign-in used by other products or cancel store subscriptions. Contact contact@example.invalid about shared-identity deletion. Provider-level backup and log retention, and the final shared-account deletion process, require verification before public release.

This notice describes the private preview. Public distribution requires finalized legal operator details, retention terms, deletion procedures, and a publicly accessible policy outside the private hosting gate.
